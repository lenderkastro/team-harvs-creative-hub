# Team Harvs Landing Page

## Goal
Create a polished public landing page that introduces Team Harvs as a fashion-forward creative collective and leads members toward the future private workspace.

## What will be built
- A strong editorial opening section with Team Harvs as the main focus, concise brand positioning, and a member workspace call-to-action.
- A visual manifesto built around “Think. Create. Learn. Execute. Grow.”
- Focus areas for fashion, editorials, campaigns, model development, and creative production.
- A concise community/about section grounded in Uyo and Akwa Ibom, with room for broader collaboration.
- A refined closing call-to-action and footer.
- Responsive navigation and layouts for mobile and desktop.

## Visual direction
- Deep burgundy and warm cream palette.
- Fashion-publication typography with a bold editorial serif paired with a clean modern sans-serif.
- Generous whitespace, sharp grid layouts, restrained borders, and layered full-bleed photography.
- Minimal, confident motion that respects reduced-motion preferences.
- No corporate dashboard styling on the public landing page.

## Content and assets
- Generate original fashion-editorial imagery suited to a young Nigerian creative collective.
- Create a typographic Team Harvs wordmark and TH submark treatment within the page.
- Use only the brand information supplied; no invented contact details, dates, or statistics.

## Technical details
- Replace the placeholder home page at `/`.
- Define reusable semantic color, typography, spacing, and motion tokens in the global design system.
- Add page-specific search and social metadata.
- Keep the future admin/member workspace out of scope for this phase; the workspace button will be presented as an upcoming entry point rather than a working login.
- Verify the page visually at desktop and mobile sizes and confirm the project builds cleanly.
