# Team Harvs Creative Hub

TEAM HARVS — WEBSITE BRIEF

You can send this directly to the website designer:

I want the Team Harvs website to function as a private creative workspace where I can manage the team, assign tasks, share resources, and monitor members’ participation.

1. Admin Dashboard — for me

Create and publish assignments/tasks.

Set tasks for a day, week, or month.

Add task descriptions, deadlines and instructions.

Upload reference materials, moodboards, images and videos.

See all active and completed assignments.

See who has viewed/opened each task.

See who has started/completed/submitted.

See who has not yet viewed or submitted.

View every member’s submission directly from the dashboard.

Submissions should support photos, videos and other relevant files.

Be able to review submissions and leave feedback/comments.

2. Member Dashboard — for Team Harvs members

Each member should have their own account/profile.

They can see tasks assigned to them.

Tasks should show the deadline and status.

They can open the task, read the instructions and view uploaded references/videos.

They can upload their completed work directly to the website.

They should be able to see whether their submission was received and any feedback from the admin.

3. Task Status/Tracking Each assignment should have clear statuses such as:

Not viewed

Viewed

In progress

Submitted

Reviewed

Completed

4. Creative Workspace The website should also serve as a central workspace where I can upload:

Moodboards

Creative references

Tutorial/reference videos

Briefs

Images

Documents

Team announcements

The goal is to have one place where Team Harvs receives assignments, gets creative direction, accesses resources, submits work, and receives feedback, while I can monitor the entire workflow from the admin dashboard.

TEAM HARVS — BRAND BRIEF

1. Brand Name

Team Harvs

2. About the Brand

Team Harvs is a creative team built around collaboration, experimentation, learning and intentional creative production. It brings together creatives across fashion, editorial and commercial production to work on projects, develop their skills, build portfolios and create meaningful visual work.

3. Vision

To build a strong creative community where young creatives can step outside their comfort zones, develop their abilities, collaborate and gain real-world creative experience.

4. What We Do

Team Harvs works across:

Fashion

Editorials

Commercial projects

Campaigns

Model development

Creative productions

Creative collaborations

5. Target Audience

Young and emerging creatives, particularly:

Models

Stylists

Makeup artists

Videographers/BTS creators

Creative assistants

Other creatives who may be brought in for specific projects

Primarily starting in Uyo/Akwa Ibom, with room to expand through collaborations.

6. Brand Personality

Team Harvs should feel:

Creative

Fashion-forward

Intentional

Youthful

Collaborative

Experimental

Confident

Warm

Modern

It should not feel overly corporate, rigid, childish, or intimidating.

7. Brand Philosophy

Think. Create. Learn. Execute. Grow.

The team isn't just about getting people to execute instructions. Members are encouraged to bring ideas, contribute value, experiment and develop their creative abilities.

8. Visual Direction

The visual identity should feel minimal, editorial and sophisticated, with inspiration from fashion publications and modern creative studios.

Primary colours:

Deep Burgundy

Cream / Off-white

The design should make good use of whitespace, strong typography, clean layouts and photography.

9. Logo/Brand Assets

Provide:

Team Harvs main logo

TH submark

Horizontal/watermark version

Burgundy version

Cream/white version

10. Website Purpose

The website will serve as the team's creative workspace, where members can receive assignments, access creative resources, submit work and receive feedback, while the admin can monitor participation and manage the team's workflow.
